ABOUT

About.

Remote sensing scientist with a background in agriculture and forest science, specializing in Earth observation, forest monitoring and open scientific software. Research experience includes terrestrial, airborne and spaceborne Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR), radar, optical imagery, vegetation mapping, forest structure, biomass and carbon dynamics, and machine learning, deep learning and artificial intelligence algorithms.

BSc in Agricultural Sciences from Catholic University Sedes Sapientiae, Peru (2013). Awarded a merit-based scholarship through the university to pursue graduate studies in Italy. MSc in Forest and Environmental Sciences and Technologies from the University of Molise (2016), with a final grade of 110/110. PhD in Sciences, Technologies, and Biotechnologies for Sustainability from the University of Molise and the University of Tuscia (2021). Doctoral dissertation recognized with the Michele Stanca Prize for the best PhD thesis at the XVIII AISSA Annual Conference (2022).

Current postdoctoral research at the University of Florida and previous postdoctoral experience at the University of Molise. Teaching experience includes university courses and practical workshops on remote sensing, Google Earth Engine and LiDAR systems, together with mentoring and co-supervision of master’s students.

Fieldwork qualifications and training

Remote Pilot Certificate, UAS Open Category A1/A3, obtained in Italy through ENAC in 2022, under the EASA framework.

Specialization in Forest Inventories, Modules I–II: Design, Planning and Field Data Collection. National Forestry and Wildlife Service (SERFOR, Ministry of Agriculture) and National University of the Peruvian Amazon, Loreto, Peru, 2018.

CESAR IVAN ALVITES DIAZ, PhD

Earth observation and forest monitoring.
Understanding natural and human-driven change.

Research focused on monitoring forest ecosystems and their responses to natural and human-driven land change. Multisensor observations, including LiDAR, radar and optical imagery, are combined with geospatial foundation-model representations, machine learning and deep learning to study forest structure, biomass and carbon dynamics over time.
